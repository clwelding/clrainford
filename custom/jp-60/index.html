Internal Shop Traveler · JP-60

JP-60 Commercial Jerk Pit — Shop Build

33 steps from receiving steel to customer handoff. Every step has a pre-task safety gate, hazard controls, PPE, a QC gate and the OSHA standards that apply. Work is signed off step by step, and the whole traveler prints for the job file.

Safety & OSHA Program Crew Mode (voice) Hot-Work Permit Pre-Shift Checklist
Safety gates are not optional. The work steps in a card stay locked until its pre-task safety gate is checked, and a step can be signed off only when the gate, every work step and the QC gate are complete. Dimensions are nominal shop baselines; the lead fabricator verifies them against the as-built pit and the customer’s local code before cutting.

Progress and sign-offs save in this browser only. Use Export / Import to move the traveler between devices, and Print to file the signed copy.
