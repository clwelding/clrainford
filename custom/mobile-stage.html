Internal Build Board · Design Spec 02

Mobile Festival Stage — Build Guide & Kanban

A 40 ft × 24 ft towable stage with a 2–4 ft adjustable deck, seven-panel folding deck, powered roof (15 ft+ clearance) and 3,600 lb of suspended audio. Work the steps in order, gather each part and tool, and move each card across the board as it is done.

Preliminary — not released for construction, structural procurement, lifting, or public use. Steps marked Hold must not start until their release gate is signed off by the responsible engineer. No thickness, rating, pin size, ballast mass, or axle count is implied here — every dimension comes from the released drawings. Unknowns are open issues, never zero loads.

Progress saves in this browser only. Use Export / Import to share the board between crew devices.
