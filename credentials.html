Certified

City of Chicago — Minority-Owned Business Enterprise (MBE)

Open PDF Download

Certified by the City of Chicago Department of Procurement Services. The certification stays in effect as long as we meet eligibility requirements and file our annual No-Change Affidavit (anniversary date: May 1). We are listed in the City’s Directory of Minority and Women-Owned Business Enterprises under:

Welding, on-site, contractors Ornamental metalwork installation Stairway, metal, installation Balcony, metal, installation Deck & grate (except roof), metal, installation

All under NAICS 238190. MBE credit toward City goals applies to self-performed work in these specialty areas.
