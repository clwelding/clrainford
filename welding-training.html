The program

What you’ll learn

Mild-steel MIG (GMAW) welding, shop safety, measurement, reading basic drawings and weld symbols, material preparation, cutting and grinding, fit-up, weld inspection and a small fabrication project — plus employment preparation and an individual next-step plan. We teach one process well rather than five badly.

Where the 180 hours go

111 h Supervised welding & fabrication lab 30 h Technical instruction 14 h Employment preparation 10 h OSHA 10 (authorized provider) 5 h First Aid / CPR / AED 10 h Breaks & transitions

Lab hours include setup, cutting, grinding, fit-up, inspection and practical assessment — they are not all arc-on time. Every learner’s booth time is logged.

Credentials you can earn

Straight talk about what each one means

Several different documents get called “certifications.” We spell out who issues each one and what it actually proves. Final offerings will be confirmed before enrollment.

CredentialPlanWho issues it & what it takes
Program completion certificateCoreC.L. Rainford, after published attendance and competency requirements. A skills transcript lists the processes, materials and positions you were actually assessed on. Not an AWS credential.
OSHA 10 General Industry cardCoreAn OSHA-authorized Outreach trainer. A completion card showing safety training, not a certification and not a substitute for employer-specific training.
First Aid / CPR / AEDPlannedA recognized provider such as the American Red Cross, with hands-on skills assessment.
AWS Certified WelderIf readyYou test at an outside AWS Accredited Test Facility, where a Certified Welding Inspector examines a sound weld, and AWS reviews the records. The test scope is set with the facility and employers. Passing is individual and not guaranteed, and keeping the credential active takes six-month maintenance filings.
NCCER modules or AWS SENSEPhase twoRequire an approved program and their own assessments. Not part of the first cohort.

Program completion does not guarantee certification, a job interview or employment.

Eligibility & fairness

Who it’s for

Adults 18 or older who can attend the schedule, perform essential shop tasks (with reasonable accommodation), follow safety rules and are legally permitted to attend the location. No prior welding experience is required. A short measurement and reading check is used to plan your support, not to screen you out.

How we treat applicants

  • No blanket exclusion because of a conviction
  • Supervision restrictions and site-access limits are worked out individually
  • Admission to training is separate from any employer’s hiring decision
  • Confidential accommodation requests and an accessible grievance process
  • You never have to describe your record to us on a public form

What we won’t promise

  • A job, an interview, or a particular wage
  • That you’ll pass an AWS test — readiness is decided together
  • Free seats before sponsorship is actually secured
  • Support services we haven’t confirmed
  • That this replaces a longer apprenticeship or college program

FAQ

Questions we expect

Do I need welding experience?

No. Week one is safety, and the first month builds from measuring and material preparation up to your first fillet welds.

Is it free? Is it paid?

Not yet confirmed. We start small — just three seats in the first cohort — so every learner gets real one-on-one time at the booth. We won’t advertise free seats until sponsorship or funding is secured. Costs, PPE arrangements and any pay will be posted before enrollment opens.

Why MIG and not stick or TIG?

Mild-steel MIG (GMAW) is a common entry-level production process and can be taught well in 12 weeks. Stick (SMAW) and flux-core can be introduced, but mastering them takes more time. If employers we interview need a different process, we will redesign the program around it.

Will I be AWS certified at the end?

Not automatically. You earn a program completion certificate. If you demonstrate readiness, you can pursue a specified AWS performance test at an outside Accredited Test Facility. Passing is up to you on the day, and the test scope is agreed in advance.

Does a record keep me out?

No blanket exclusion. Specific supervision or site-access restrictions are handled individually. Please don’t put offense details in the form below — we’ll talk privately.

The class starts at 4 PM. What if I work days?

That is a real barrier, and we’d rather hear it now. Tell us in the form below so we can plan the schedule around the people who actually want to attend.

When does it start?

Dates aren’t set. Join the interest list and you’ll hear first.
