Entry-Level · No Experience Required

Welder Trainee

Build a career in the trade from the ground up. Train under experienced welders, learn shop safety and core weld processes, and work toward AWS certification — no prior welding experience needed.

LocationChicago, IL
Reports ToShop Foreman / Lead Welder
Also Acceptable TitleApprentice Welder

The Role

An entry-level position for someone with little or no professional welding experience who wants to build a career in the trade. You'll work under direct supervision of experienced welders, learning shop safety, material handling, and basic weld processes, with a defined path toward independent welding and AWS certification. This position is designed to train from the ground up — no prior welding certification is required.

Core Responsibilities

  • Learn and follow all shop safety procedures (PPE, ventilation, fire watch, equipment lockout)
  • Assist journeyman/lead welders with fit-up, clamping, and material positioning
  • Perform grinding, cleaning, and surface prep on parts before and after welding
  • Learn to read basic blueprints/weld symbols under supervision
  • Practice and develop skill in core processes (typically starting with MIG, progressing to stick and/or TIG depending on shop needs)
  • Perform tack welds and non-critical welds under direct supervision, progressing to independent work as skill develops
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and safe work area
  • Handle material movement, inventory pulls, and basic tool maintenance
  • Attend any in-house or external training toward AWS certification as directed

What We're Looking For

  • No prior welding experience required; a demonstrated interest in the trade (shop class, hobbyist experience, mechanical aptitude) is a plus
  • High school diploma or GED preferred, not required
  • Ability to lift 50+ lbs, stand for extended periods, and work in a shop environment (heat, noise, fumes with proper PPE)
  • Reliable attendance and willingness to take direction — this role is evaluated as much on coachability as on raw skill
  • Basic math skills (fractions, measurement, angles) sufficient to read a tape measure and basic prints
  • Willingness to work toward AWS certification within a defined training period (commonly 6–12 months, set by the shop)

What the Shop Provides

  • Structured on-the-job training under experienced welders
  • A defined path to Welder I / Journeyman status with wage increases tied to skill milestones
  • Support pursuing AWS D1.1 (or relevant process) certification, including exam cost coverage after the probationary period
  • PPE and safety equipment provided

Advancement Path

Trainee→ Welder I→ Welder II / Journeyman

Pay and responsibility increase at each stage as certifications and independent capability are demonstrated.

Compensation

Entry-level trainee pay, with defined raises at 90 days, 6 months, and upon first certification — clear milestones so you know exactly what you're working toward.
