We're Hiring

Government & Corporate Contracts Specialist

Own the full cradle-to-grave process of finding, qualifying, bidding, winning, and maintaining contract relationships across federal, state, City of Chicago, corporate, and industrial channels for a growing Chicago fabrication shop.

LocationChicago, IL
Reports ToOwner / General Manager
Alternate TitleBusiness Development & Contracts Acquisition Manager

The Role

This is a hybrid business-development and compliance role — half hunting for and qualifying contract opportunities, half maintaining the certifications, registrations, and documentation that make the shop eligible to win them. You'll work across five distinct channels: federal (SAM.gov, GSA, VA), state of Illinois (BidBuy, CDB, IDOT), City of Chicago and Cook County, corporate supplier diversity (NMSDC), and direct industrial/facility maintenance contracts.

Core Responsibilities

  • Certification & registration maintenance — keep SAM.gov, City MBE/WBE/DBE, Illinois BEP, NMSDC, and facility-specific vendor prequalifications current; file recertifications before they lapse.
  • Lead generation — monitor SAM.gov, state procurement bulletins, the City's Bid Opportunity List, the NMSDC Hub, and direct facility/GC relationships.
  • Opportunity qualification — triage each lead against shop capacity, true capability match, and realistic win probability before committing bid-prep time.
  • Bid/proposal preparation — assemble capability statements, past performance references, pricing, and required forms.
  • Relationship management — maintain an active target list of GC estimators, plant maintenance managers, and procurement contacts through regular direct outreach.
  • Pipeline ownership — track every opportunity from source through outcome, with clear follow-up dates.
  • Compliance during execution — ensure required reporting (certified payroll, utilization/payment confirmations) goes out on time.

What We're Looking For

  • 2+ years in business development, estimating, or contracts administration — construction/industrial/fabrication industry strongly preferred.
  • Working familiarity with public procurement processes (federal, state, or municipal) is a strong plus; willingness to learn quickly is a requirement if not already familiar.
  • Comfortable with government/corporate web portals and basic document preparation (capability statements, COIs, bid forms).
  • Strong organizational discipline — this role lives and dies by deadlines and recertification dates.
  • Relationship-building instincts — a meaningful share of this job is direct outreach and follow-up, not just monitoring portals.

Why This Role Matters

The shop is already MBE/WBE/DBE certified through the City of Chicago — real leverage across state, city, and corporate channels that most competitors don't have. This role is about putting that leverage to work.

How Success Is Measured

Certifications maintained without lapse, qualified opportunities bid per quarter, win rate by channel, revenue sourced by channel, and approved-vendor status across target industrial facilities.
